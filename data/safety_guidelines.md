# Mumzworld Product Safety Guidelines

## Age-Based Safety Rules

### Newborn (0-3 months)
- Products must be BPA-free for feeding items
- No loose parts or detachable small components
- Fabrics must be hypoallergenic and breathable
- Car seats must be rear-facing only
- Swaddles should be used until baby shows signs of rolling

### Infant (3-12 months)
- No toys with small parts (choking hazard for items < 3.5cm)
- All toys must meet EN 71 or ASTM F963 safety standards
- High chairs require 5-point harness
- Button batteries are extremely dangerous — supervision required
- Strollers need recline capability for younger infants

### Toddler (12-36 months)
- Choking hazard: avoid marbles, small balls, items under 3.5cm diameter
- Toys with magnets must be avoided for children under 3
- Furniture must be tip-proof or wall-anchored
- Car seats can transition to forward-facing at 15 months / 76cm minimum

### Preschool (36-60 months)
- Small LEGO-style bricks (non-Duplo) appropriate from 4 years
- Scissors must be safety/blunt-tip only
- Battery compartments must be screw-secured

### School Age (60+ months)
- Science kits with chemicals require adult supervision
- Electronics should have volume-limiting features
- Age ratings on puzzles and games should be followed

## General Safety Rules
1. **Weight limits are absolute** — never exceed manufacturer's stated maximum weight
2. **Age minimums are absolute** — never recommend a product below its minimum age
3. **Choking hazard items** — NEVER suitable for children under 36 months
4. **Button batteries** — extreme danger for all children; adult supervision always required
5. **Car seat certifications** — must have ECE R44/04 or i-Size R129 for GCC
6. **When in doubt, recommend the safer option** — prioritize child safety over convenience
7. **Recalled products** — if any product has a recall notice, immediately flag it

## Uncertainty Protocol
- If product specifications are incomplete, state uncertainty explicitly
- If age range is ambiguous, recommend for the OLDER age boundary
- Never extrapolate safety data from similar products — each product is unique
